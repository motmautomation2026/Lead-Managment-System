# REL-1 go-live runbook (production, 18 Nov 2026)

**Who runs it:** Govinda only. Claude Code never touches production.
**What goes live:** the CRM plus the AI. New leads (created after step 5) are worked automatically. Old leads are not,
until an admin or the lead's owner switches automation on (one contact, or many from the Contacts list).
**Code:** branch `dev` at the commit in the release report. API, worker and frontend all from **that one commit**.
**Total:** about 2½ hours plus the shadow check. Read **07-kill-switch-and-rollback.md first** (2 min). Keep it open.

How to read each step: **Do** → ✅ **OK when** → ⛔ **STOP if** (stop = do not continue; post what you see) → ⏱ time.

## Things decided for today (do not change without management)
- AI decision cap **10 / day per workspace**; follow-up limits, WhatsApp safety, send window, fact check and
  rewrite-else-handoff unchanged.
- **Off** until management approves the texts: meeting reminders to leads, the "We tried calling" WhatsApp (both set off
  by step 5), email open tracking (off).
- A lead is never in the AI and a workflow at the same time: going live removes AI-live contacts from workflows, and a
  live contact is never auto-enrolled in one.
